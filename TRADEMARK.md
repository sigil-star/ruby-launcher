# Trademark & branding

The **source code** of Ruby Launcher is available under the [LICENSE](LICENSE)
(PolyForm Strict 1.0.0). That license covers the *code*. It does **not** give you any right
to the Ruby Launcher **name, brand, or look**.

## Not licensed

The following are **not** covered by the source license and remain reserved:

- The names **"Ruby"** and **"Ruby Launcher"** (and confusingly similar names).
- The Ruby logo and app icon (`src-tauri/assets/`, `public/RubyLauncher.png`, and related art).
- The "Ruby" wordmark, colours, and overall visual identity.
- The `dsk.wtf` domain and the official update feed.

## What this means

- **You may not** release a launcher, fork, or derivative that calls itself "Ruby",
  "Ruby Launcher", or anything likely to be confused with it, or that uses the Ruby logo
  or branding.
- **You may** say that your plugin or project "works with Ruby Launcher" or "is built for
  Ruby Launcher" — plain, factual references are fine.
- If you build something of your own on top of Ruby's plugin API, give it **your own** name
  and icon.

## Official builds

Only builds signed by the project and distributed from **dsk.wtf** are genuine Ruby
Launcher. The update signature key is private and never ships in the app, so no one else
can publish an update that Ruby will accept. A build from anywhere else is not Ruby, even
if it looks like it.

Questions about name/branding use: open an issue before shipping.
